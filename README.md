# Empirical Research Methods for Operations Management

A practical research handbook on empirical research methods commonly used
in Operations Management (OM).

## Contents

### 1. Handbook of Empirical Research Methods for Operations Management

Covers:

- Organizational theories
- Secondary-data research
- Event studies
- Selection bias
- Difference-in-differences
- Endogeneity
- Instrumental variables
- Panel-data methods
- GMM
- Quasi-experimental research
- Survey research
- Reliability and validity
- Structural equation modeling
- Common method bias
- Moderation and mediation

[Download the Handbook](./Handbook-of-Empirical-Research-Methods-for-OM.pdf)

### 2. Discussion of Endogeneity in OM Empirical Research

A focused methodological note on endogeneity in empirical OM research,
including:

- Sources of endogeneity
- Research-design remedies
- Instrumental variables
- Panel-data approaches
- Quasi-experimental approaches
- Statistical remedies

[Download the Endogeneity Note](./Endogeneity-in-OM-Empirical-Research.pdf)
